The whole system, on one page. The bird and the wordmark are generated by src/logo.py; the colours come from src/tokens.css; the contrast ratios below are recomputed in this page every time it loads, so a colour that gets nudged and quietly stops meeting AA turns red here instead of staying green in a comment.

The mark

A wren: compact body, fine beak, and the short tail cocked up over the back. The tail is the whole identity — it is what separates this from a generic songbird — so it survives in every reduction below.

Reduction

The full mark carries a wing, an eye and the pale eyebrow stripe (a real wren's supercilium). Below roughly 24 px those stop resolving and start reading as dirt, so anything smaller uses the simple mark — one colour, silhouette only. The favicon already does.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space of at least 0.35× the mark's height on every side — nothing, including type, another logo or the edge of the canvas, comes inside it. Minimum size for the full mark is 20 px; below that, switch to the simple mark.

Colour

Clay is the brand. It is a graphic colour, not a text colour — at 3.3:1 on paper it is fine for the mark, for display type at 24 px and up, and for fills and borders, and not fine for body copy. Use --w-clay-ink when clay has to carry small text or sit under cream. Every ratio below is measured live.

Type

The wordmark ships as outlines, so nothing has to match it exactly. Running text is a humanist sans that degrades to the system UI font — a local-first assistant that phones a CDN for a typeface would be missing its own point. Scale is a 1.25 major third.
